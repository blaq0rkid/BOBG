Federal Diagnostic

Know Where You Stand Before You Compete

The Problem Most Companies Face

Most companies enter the federal market blind. They don't know their P-Win (Probability of Win). They don't know their compliance gaps. They don't know which agencies align with their capabilities. They just know they want federal contracts—and they waste years chasing opportunities they have no realistic chance of winning.

The Federal Diagnostic eliminates this guesswork. It's a rigorous assessment that tells you exactly where you stand, what gaps you need to close, and the realistic timeline for federal market entry.

What's Included in Your Federal Diagnostic

Federal Readiness Assessment

Comprehensive evaluation of your current federal market position, including SAM.gov registration status, NAICS code alignment, and market positioning.

Compliance Infrastructure Analysis

Detailed gap analysis of your DCAA accounting system, cybersecurity compliance (CMMC/NIST 800-171), and regulatory requirements specific to your target agencies.

Past Performance Evaluation

Assessment of how your commercial track record translates to federal credibility, including identification of transferable capabilities and positioning strategies.

Financial Resilience Review

Evaluation of your working capital and financial capacity to sustain 90-120 day federal payment cycles and invest in federal market development.

Set-Aside Eligibility Analysis

Review of your eligibility for small business set-asides (8(a), WOSB, SDVOSB, HUBZone) and strategic recommendations for certification priorities.

Prioritized Remediation Roadmap

Clear, actionable plan outlining the specific steps required to close identified gaps, ranked by priority and impact on your federal readiness.

Agency Alignment Preliminary Analysis

Initial identification of federal agencies that align with your capabilities and mission, providing direction for deeper strategic engagement.

Realistic Timeline for Market Entry

Honest assessment of how long it will take to become truly competitive in the federal market, based on your current position and resource commitment.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does the diagnostic process take?

Typically 2-4 weeks from intake to final report delivery, depending on the complexity of your organization and information availability.

What if I'm not ready for federal contracting?

That's exactly what the diagnostic reveals. We'll tell you honestly if you're not ready and provide the specific steps required to become ready. No false promises.

Do I need to commit to further services?

No. The Federal Diagnostic is a standalone engagement. What you do with the findings is entirely up to you.

What information do I need to provide?

You'll complete a comprehensive intake form covering your company profile, capabilities, compliance status, financial capacity, and federal market objectives. The more detailed your responses, the more precise our assessment.

Is this only for companies with no federal experience?

No. The diagnostic is valuable for companies at various stages: exploring entry, operating as subcontractors, or even established contractors seeking to expand into new agencies.
